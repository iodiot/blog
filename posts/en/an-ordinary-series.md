Curious: take the sum\
**S = 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 32 + ...**,\
and multiply it by two:\
**2S = 2 + 4 + 8 + 32 + 64 + ...  => 2S = S - 1**,\
and we get that our sum **S** equals **-1**.

So **1 + 2 + 4 + ...** is an infinitely precise representation of **-1** in a binary computer.

*P.S.: Of course, this is no paradox at all — you just have to handle sums properly.*
